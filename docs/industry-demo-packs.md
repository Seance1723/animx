# Industry Demo Packs (v3.14.0)

Industry packs provide vertical, page-level guidance on which section patterns to stack together.

## Available Packs
- **SaaS / Startup**: Stacks `hero-premium-soft`, `feature-grid-stagger`, and `pricing-cards`.
- **Dashboard / Admin**: Focuses on `dashboard-kpi-grid` and quick layout reveals.
- **Ecommerce**: Heavy on `ecommerce-product-detail-motion` and zoom reveals.
- **Blog / Documentation**: Focuses on sidebar reveals and content scrolling.
