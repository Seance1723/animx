# Section Animation Patterns

AnimX defines patterns at the Section level, ensuring that components operate cohesively.

### Example: SaaS Hero Premium Soft
Combines `text-mask-up` on the `<h1>`, a `fade-up` on the `<p>`, and a `button-glow-soft` on the CTA.
By applying a timeline stagger to these atomic presets, the section loads perfectly in sequence.
