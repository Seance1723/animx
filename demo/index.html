Quick Start

Drop in the CDN links and start animating instantly via data attributes or JavaScript.

<link rel="stylesheet" href="animx.min.css"> <script src="animx.min.js"></script> <div data-ax="fade-up">I will animate on load or scroll!</div>

Accessibility & Reduced Motion

      
AnimX deeply respects prefers-reduced-motion. When enabled, heavy transforms and delays are stripped, resolving to their final states instantly to protect users, while retaining critical focus and semantic layout.
